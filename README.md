# 🧩 AI Puzzle Game

Welcome to **AI Puzzle Game** — a fun, category-based puzzle challenge where your goal is to solve puzzles, track your score, and improve with every round.  

---

## 🎮 How to Play
1. **Choose a Category**  
   Start by selecting from available puzzle categories. Each one offers a different type of challenge.

2. **Solve the Puzzle**  
   Use your knowledge and problem-solving skills to complete the puzzle.  
   - Hints may be available depending on the category.  
   - Each puzzle has only one correct solution.

3. **Track Your Score**  
   Every correct answer adds to your score. Wrong attempts may reduce your points or end the round.

4. **Check Your Stats**  
   After each round, view your performance stats including total puzzles solved, accuracy, and best streak.

---

## 🏆 Features
- Multiple puzzle categories to test different skills
